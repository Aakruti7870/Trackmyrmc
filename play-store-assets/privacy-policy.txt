CONCRETE KING (operated by GOLD-e Tech, the "Company", "we", "us") provides a
Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) marketplace and plant-management platform at
trackmyrmc.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we
use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, app, or WhatsApp
notifications, you agree to the practices described here.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
• Account & contact details — name, phone number, company name, GST details, and
  email where provided.
• Order & delivery data — concrete grade, quantity, delivery address, order
  history, challans, and dispatch records.
• Location data — site/delivery coordinates and, for tracking, the live location
  of transit vehicles servicing your order.
• Usage & device data — log data such as IP address and basic device/browser
  information used to operate and secure the service.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
• To create and manage your account and orders.
• To dispatch concrete, generate challans, and enable live delivery tracking.
• To send order and delivery updates (including via WhatsApp/SMS).
• To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
• To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

3. SHARING
We share information only as needed to run the service — for example with the RMC
plant fulfilling your order, transport/driver personnel for delivery, and service
providers (such as messaging, hosting, and maps). We do not sell your personal
information.

4. DATA RETENTION
We keep information for as long as your account is active and as required for
legal, tax, and business records, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

5. SECURITY
Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is restricted by role.
No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your
information.

6. YOUR CHOICES & RIGHTS
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and
you may opt out of non-essential messages. Contact us using the details below.

7. CHILDREN
The service is intended for businesses and adults; it is not directed at children
under 18.

8. CHANGES
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on
this page with a new "Last updated" date.
