FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------
CONCRETE KING is the all-in-one platform for ready-mix concrete (RMC) — connecting customers with nearby approved plants and giving plant teams complete control of their operations from a single screen.

FOR CUSTOMERS
• Discover approved RMC plants near your site on a live map
• Compare plants and request the grade you need (M20, M25, M30, M35, M40, M50 and more)
• Place and manage concrete orders in a few taps
• Track dispatch and delivery in real time
• Get instant alerts when your order is dispatched and delivered
• View delivery challans and proof-of-delivery photos

FOR RMC PLANTS & STAFF
• Manage orders, dispatch and challan generation end to end
• Live GPS tracking of your transit-mixer fleet
• Concrete freshness / pour-by countdown so loads are placed on time
• Batch production records and mix-design management by grade
• Client and vehicle management
• Dashboards and reports for output, on-time rate and fleet status

WHY CONCRETE KING
Built ground-up for the ready-mix concrete industry, CONCRETE KING brings ordering, live tracking, freshness monitoring and fleet intelligence together in one simple app — helping customers get quality concrete on time and helping plants run a tighter, more profitable operation.

Order. Track. Deliver. In real time.
