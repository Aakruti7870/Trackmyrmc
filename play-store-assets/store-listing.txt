FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------
CONCRETE KING is a complete Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) marketplace and plant
management platform. Customers can discover nearby approved RMC plants and place
orders in minutes, while plant teams manage operations, dispatch, fleet and
finances from a single premium dashboard.

FOR CUSTOMERS
• Discover approved RMC plants near your site on a live map.
• Place concrete orders by grade and quantity in a few taps.
• Track your order and the live location of the transit mixer on the way.
• View delivery challans and your full order history.
• Get instant updates on WhatsApp.

FOR PLANTS & STAFF
• Orders: create, edit and track dispatch progress for every order.
• Dispatch: generate delivery challans and mark deliveries complete.
• Fleet: manage transit mixers, drivers and vehicle status.
• Batch Report: log every concrete batch with grade, quantity and operator.
• Mix Design: maintain cement/water/sand/aggregate proportions per grade.
• Reports: daily trends, grade and client breakdowns, fuel reconciliation and
  CSV exports.
• Demand Forecast, Shift Reports, Fuel Log and Activity / Audit logs.
• Role-based access for admins, dispatchers, plant operators, clients and drivers.

WHY CONCRETE KING
• Built specifically for the ready-mix concrete industry.
• Clean, fast, premium interface that works on phone and desktop.
• Real-time order tracking and dispatch visibility end to end.
• Secure sign-in by phone (one-time code) or email.

Whether you run a batching plant or order concrete for your site, CONCRETE KING
keeps your operations, dispatch and deliveries organised in one place.
