b-roll mixed with real app screen-recordings**, and includes voiceover, on-screen
text, and **strong, impactful background-music** direction.

## Shared production spec (applies to every script)

- **Brand palette:** teal-green `#178a6e` (primary), deep teal `#0f2e29` (text/dark),
  emerald `#1f9e80` (accent glow), concrete grey `#9aa3a0`, off-white `#eaf6f1`.
- **Typography:** Display — *Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold* / *Anton*; Body & UI — *Inter*.
- **Music:** Cinematic, uplifting, "confident-tech" — soft pulsing pad under narration,
  a clean rhythmic beat during steps, a warm resolve on the recap. Keep it under the
  VO (-18 to -22 LUFS bed) so instructions stay clear.
- **Voice:** Calm, friendly, instructional. Indian-English friendly. ~140–150 wpm.
- **Format:** 16:9 master + 9:16 vertical crop for mobile help.
- **Style rule:** Cinematic AI b-roll sets the mood; **real screen-recordings carry the
  actual steps** so viewers can follow exactly. Always show finger-tap / cursor cues and
  highlight the field being used.

## AI video generation — global prompt style

Append to every b-roll prompt for consistency:
> "cinematic, shallow depth of field, soft volumetric morning light, teal-green color
> grade, realistic, 4k, no on-screen text, no logos, no readable signage, smooth slow
> camera move."

(Generate text/overlays in your editor — never bake words into AI footage.)
