# Tutorial 03 — Plant Staff Sign-In (Email / WhatsApp OTP)

**Audience:** Plant staff — Admin, Dispatcher, Plant Operator, Accountant, Sales
**Runtime target:** 4:00–4:45
**Goal:** Teach provisioned staff to sign in with their work email and a one-time code.

> Spec inherited from `README.md`. Music: focused, professional, "control-room" energy.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:25)
- **AI B-roll:** Cinematic interior of a modern batching-plant control room at dawn;
  screens glowing teal, a dispatcher walking in with coffee. Slow dolly. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `Plant staff access — secure, passwordless sign-in`
- **VO:** "Running a plant means dispatch, fleet, and quality all moving at once. Your
  access to Concrete King is built to be secure and quick — no passwords to manage. Let's
  walk through staff sign-in."
- **Music:** Purposeful pad rises.

## SCENE 2 — WHO THIS IS FOR / PREREQUISITE (0:25 – 0:55)
- **Screen capture:** `/login` page → tap the **Plant Staff** tab (or open `/login?staff=1`).
- **On-screen text:** `For provisioned staff only · You'll need your WORK email`
- **VO:** "This is for staff whose accounts have already been created by your plant admin —
  Admins, Dispatchers, Operators, Accountants and Sales. You'll sign in with the work
  email your account was set up with. If you don't have access yet, ask your admin to add you."
- **Music:** Light beat enters.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: OPEN THE PLANT STAFF TAB (0:55 – 1:25)
- **Screen capture:** From the login page, select **Plant Staff**; the email field appears.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Open Sign in → "Plant Staff" tab`
- **VO:** "On the sign-in screen, switch to the Plant Staff tab. You can also reach it
  directly with the staff link your plant shares. You'll see a field for your work email."
- **Cue:** Tap-ripple on the Plant Staff tab.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: ENTER YOUR WORK EMAIL (1:25 – 2:00)
- **Screen capture:** Type the work email; tap continue. The app checks your sign-in
  method behind the scenes.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Enter your work email → Continue`
- **VO:** "Enter your work email and continue. Concrete King checks how your account is
  set up, then prepares a one-time code for you — there's no password step for standard
  staff."
- **Cue:** Highlight the email field, then Continue.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: RECEIVE YOUR CODE (2:00 – 2:40)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of an email inbox lighting up on a phone; a clean notification.
- **Screen capture:** A 6-digit code arrives by **email** (or **WhatsApp** if email
  delivery isn't configured).
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — Get your 6-digit code (email / WhatsApp)`
- **VO:** "A six-digit code is sent to you — normally by email, and by WhatsApp if your
  plant uses that instead. It's time-limited, so grab it while it's fresh."
- **Music:** Confirm "ping".

## SCENE 6 — STEP 4: ENTER CODE & SIGN IN (2:40 – 3:20)
- **Screen capture:** Enter the code; sign-in completes; land on the **staff dashboard**
  (KPIs, dispatch feed). Note other sessions get signed out for security.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 4 — Enter code → secure session starts`
- **VO:** "Type the code in, and you're in. For security, signing in here signs you out of
  other devices and starts a fresh, activity-tracked session — so an unattended screen
  won't stay open forever."
- **Cue:** Digits light teal; transition into the dashboard.

## SCENE 7 — SECURITY & TIPS (3:20 – 3:55)
- **Screen capture:** Show **Resend code**; mention idle time-out behavior.
- **On-screen text:** `Tips: codes expire · sessions idle-out · ask admin for access`
- **VO:** "A few things to remember: codes expire quickly, so use them right away;
  your session will time out when idle to keep plant data safe; and if your email isn't
  recognized, your admin simply needs to provision your account first."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 8 — RECAP & CLOSE (3:55 – 4:30)
- **AI B-roll:** The control room hums; trucks roll out past the gate.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Plant Staff tab` → `2 Work email` → `3 Get code` → `4 Enter code`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "That's staff sign-in: open the Plant Staff tab, enter your work email, get your
  code, and type it in. Now let's run the plant."
- **Music:** Confident resolve, fade.
