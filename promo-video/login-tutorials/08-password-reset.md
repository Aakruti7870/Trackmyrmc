# Tutorial 08 — Password Reset / Forgot Password

**Audience:** Any password-holding user (customers with a password, super admins)
**Runtime target:** 3:00–3:45
**Goal:** Teach a user to securely reset a forgotten password.

> Spec inherited from `README.md`. Music: calm, reassuring, "we've got you" tone.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:20)
- **AI B-roll:** A user pauses at a laptop, slightly stuck, then relaxes as light warms
  the frame — problem about to be solved. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `Forgot your password? It's a quick, secure fix`
- **VO:** "Locked out because you forgot your password? Don't worry — resetting it is quick,
  secure, and only takes a moment. Let's sort it out."
- **Music:** Calm pad rises.

## SCENE 2 — WHO THIS IS FOR (0:20 – 0:45)
- **Screen capture:** On the password step, show the **Forgot password?** link → `/forgot-password`.
- **On-screen text:** `For accounts that use a password (e.g. super admins)`
- **VO:** "This is for accounts that sign in with a password — like super admins. If you
  normally use a one-time code on your phone or email, you don't need a password at all,
  so you can skip this guide."
- **Music:** Light beat enters.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: OPEN FORGOT PASSWORD (0:45 – 1:15)
- **Screen capture:** Tap **Forgot password?**; the `/forgot-password` page opens with an
  email field.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Tap "Forgot password?"`
- **VO:** "On the password screen, tap Forgot password. You'll see a simple page asking for
  your email address."
- **Cue:** Tap-ripple on the link.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: ENTER YOUR EMAIL (1:15 – 1:55)
- **Screen capture:** Type the account email; tap **Send reset link**; a neutral
  confirmation appears (no account details revealed).
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Enter your email → Send reset link`
- **VO:** "Enter the email tied to your account and request a reset link. For your safety,
  the message is always the same whether or not the email is on file — so no one can fish
  for which accounts exist."
- **Cue:** Highlight the email field, then the button.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: OPEN THE RESET LINK (1:55 – 2:30)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of an inbox lighting up; a secure link glows teal.
- **Screen capture:** Open the email, tap the reset link; it opens the **Set Password** page.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — Open the email → tap the reset link`
- **VO:** "Check your inbox and open the reset link. It's one-time and time-limited, so use
  it promptly. It takes you to a secure Set Password page."
- **Music:** Confirm "ping".

## SCENE 6 — STEP 4: SET A NEW PASSWORD (2:30 – 3:05)
- **AI B-roll:** A padlock re-locking cleanly — security restored.
- **Screen capture:** Enter and confirm a new password; submit; you're signed in and land
  on your dashboard.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 4 — Set a new password → you're back in`
- **VO:** "Create and confirm a new password, then submit. You're signed straight back in.
  One note for safety: resetting a password won't reactivate an account that's been
  suspended — that still needs an admin."
- **Cue:** Secure "lock" click; transition into the dashboard.

## SCENE 7 — TIPS (3:05 – 3:25)
- **On-screen text:** `Didn't get the email? Check spam · confirm the address · try again`
- **VO:** "No email after a minute or two? Check your spam folder, confirm you used the
  right address, and request the link again if needed."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 8 — RECAP & CLOSE (3:25 – 3:45)
- **AI B-roll:** The user smiles, back at work, screen glowing teal.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Forgot password` → `2 Enter email` → `3 Open link` → `4 New password`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "Forgot password, enter your email, open the link, set a new one — and you're
  back in. Secure and simple, with Concrete King."
- **Music:** Reassuring resolve, fade.
