# Tutorial 01 — Customer Phone Sign-In (WhatsApp / SMS OTP)

**Audience:** Customers / clients buying concrete
**Runtime target:** 3:30–4:00
**Goal:** Teach a customer to sign in with their mobile number and a one-time code.

> Spec (palette, music, voice) inherited from `README.md`. Music: warm, confident,
> light percussion under the step section; soft pad under intro & recap.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:20)
- **AI B-roll:** A customer on a busy construction site pulls out a phone at golden
  hour; behind them a transit mixer rotates. Slow push-in. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `Sign in in seconds — just your phone number`
- **VO:** "Ordering ready-mix concrete should be as simple as sending a message. With
  Concrete King, signing in takes nothing but your phone number. Let's do it together."
- **Music:** Soft cinematic pad rises.

## SCENE 2 — WHO THIS IS FOR / WHAT YOU NEED (0:20 – 0:40)
- **AI B-roll:** Clean close-up of a hand holding a phone; WhatsApp notification glow.
- **Screen capture:** App splash → the `/login` page with the **Customer** tab active.
- **On-screen text:** `You'll need: your mobile number + WhatsApp or SMS`
- **VO:** "This is for customers. All you need is the mobile number you want to use, and
  access to WhatsApp or text messages on that number. No password to remember."
- **Music:** Beat gently enters.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: OPEN THE LOGIN PAGE (0:40 – 1:05)
- **Screen capture:** Land on the app, tap **Sign in**, the **Customer** tab is selected
  by default. Highlight the tab.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Open Sign in → "Customer" tab`
- **VO:** "Open the app and tap Sign in. By default you'll land on the Customer tab —
  that's exactly where you want to be."
- **Cue:** Animated tap-ripple on the Customer tab.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: ENTER YOUR MOBILE NUMBER (1:05 – 1:40)
- **Screen capture:** Type a mobile number into the phone field. If first-time, the
  **Name** field appears — fill it in. Tap **Send code**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Enter mobile number (+ name if you're new)`
- **VO:** "Type in your mobile number. If this is your first time, you'll also be asked
  for your name so we know who you are. Then tap Send code."
- **Cue:** Highlight the phone field, then the Send code button.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: RECEIVE THE CODE (1:40 – 2:15)
- **AI B-roll:** Cinematic macro of a phone buzzing, a message preview sliding down.
- **Screen capture:** A 6-digit code arrives via **WhatsApp** (primary) or **SMS**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — Get your 6-digit code on WhatsApp or SMS`
- **VO:** "Within a few seconds, a six-digit code arrives — usually on WhatsApp, or by
  text message. Keep it handy; it's valid for a short time only."
- **Music:** A light confirm "ping".

## SCENE 6 — STEP 4: ENTER THE CODE & SIGN IN (2:15 – 2:55)
- **Screen capture:** Type the code into the six-box OTP input; it auto-advances and
  signs in; smooth transition to the **discovery / home dashboard**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 4 — Enter the code → you're in`
- **VO:** "Enter the code in the six boxes. That's it — you're signed in, and you stay
  signed in across your sessions, so you won't have to repeat this every time."
- **Cue:** Each digit lights teal as it's typed; success check on the last box.

## SCENE 7 — TROUBLESHOOTING / TIPS (2:55 – 3:25)
- **Screen capture:** Show the **Resend code** option; show correcting a wrong number.
- **On-screen text:** `Didn't get it? → Resend · Check number · Check WhatsApp/SMS`
- **VO:** "Didn't receive a code? Make sure the number is correct, check both WhatsApp
  and your messages, and use Resend if needed. Still stuck? Reach out to the plant — they
  can help verify your number."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 8 — RECAP & CLOSE (3:25 – 4:00)
- **AI B-roll:** The customer smiles, pockets the phone; a mixer pulls out behind them.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Open Sign in` → `2 Enter number` → `3 Get code` → `4 Enter code`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "And that's the whole sign-in: open Sign in, enter your number, get your code,
  type it in. Welcome to Concrete King — let's get your concrete moving."
- **Music:** Warm resolve, fade out.
