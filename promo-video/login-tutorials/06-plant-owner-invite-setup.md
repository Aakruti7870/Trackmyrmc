# Tutorial 06 — Plant Owner Onboarding (Invite → Set Password)

**Audience:** Newly provisioned plant owners
**Runtime target:** 4:00–4:30
**Goal:** Teach an invited owner to activate their account from the invite email.

> Spec inherited from `README.md`. Music: premium, "welcome to the platform" warmth
> with a confident build.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:25)
- **AI B-roll:** Cinematic aerial of a real RMC plant at golden hour — silos, conveyor,
  trucks lined up; the owner stands proud at the gate. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `Your plant, now on Concrete King — let's activate your account`
- **VO:** "Welcome aboard. Your plant has been set up on Concrete King, and an invitation
  is waiting for you. Let's activate your owner account so you can take control."
- **Music:** Premium pad rises.

## SCENE 2 — WHO / PREREQUISITE (0:25 – 0:55)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of an email landing on a phone with a subtle teal glow.
- **On-screen text:** `You'll need: the invite email from the platform team`
- **VO:** "This is for owners whose plant was created by the platform team. Everything
  starts with the invitation email sent to you — so open your inbox and let's begin."
- **Music:** Light beat enters.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: OPEN THE INVITE LINK (0:55 – 1:35)
- **Screen capture:** Open the invite email, tap the secure activation link; it opens the
  **Set Password** page.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Open the invite email → tap the activation link`
- **VO:** "Find the invitation email and tap the activation link inside. It opens a secure
  Set Password page made just for you. This link is one-time and time-limited, so use it
  soon after you receive it."
- **Cue:** Tap-ripple on the email link.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: SET YOUR PASSWORD (1:35 – 2:25)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of a vault closing securely — ownership and trust motif.
- **Screen capture:** Enter and confirm a strong new password; tap **Set password**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Create a strong password → Set password`
- **VO:** "Create a strong password and confirm it. This secures your owner account. Choose
  something only you would know — mixing letters, numbers and symbols keeps your plant's
  data safe."
- **Music:** Confident swell; secure "lock" click.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: AUTO SIGN-IN (2:25 – 3:00)
- **Screen capture:** The link is consumed, the password is saved, and you're signed in
  automatically — landing on your **plant dashboard**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — You're activated & signed in automatically`
- **VO:** "Once you set your password, the invitation is used up and you're signed in
  automatically — straight into your plant's dashboard. Your account is now active."
- **Cue:** Smooth teal transition into the dashboard.

## SCENE 6 — IMPORTANT: HOW YOU SIGN IN NEXT TIME (3:00 – 3:45)
- **Screen capture:** Show the **Plant Staff** tab + email OTP flow (reference Tutorial 03).
- **On-screen text:** `Next time → Plant Staff tab → email + one-time code (no password)`
- **VO:** "Here's the important part: that password is just for activation. From now on, you
  sign in like all plant staff — on the Plant Staff tab, with your email and a one-time
  code. It's more secure, and there's no password to remember day to day."
- **Music:** Steady, reassuring.

## SCENE 7 — TIPS (3:45 – 4:10)
- **On-screen text:** `Link expired? Ask the platform team to re-send your invite`
- **VO:** "If your invite link has expired before you used it, no problem — just ask the
  platform team to send a fresh one."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 8 — RECAP & CLOSE (4:10 – 4:30)
- **AI B-roll:** The owner looks out over the plant as trucks roll out.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Open invite` → `2 Set password` → `3 Auto sign-in` → `Next: staff OTP`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "Open your invite, set your password, and you're activated — then it's one-time
  codes from there. Welcome to Concrete King."
- **Music:** Premium resolve, fade.
