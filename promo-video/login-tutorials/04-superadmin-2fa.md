# Tutorial 04 — Super Admin Sign-In (Password + Email 2FA)

**Audience:** Platform super admins (role: authority)
**Runtime target:** 4:00–4:30
**Goal:** Teach platform admins to sign in with password plus a second factor.

> Spec inherited from `README.md`. Music: serious, secure, "mission-control" tone.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:25)
- **AI B-roll:** Cinematic wide of a dark platform-operations room, world-map style
  wall of plant locations glowing teal; a single admin at a clean desk. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `Platform Super Admin — protected by two-factor sign-in`
- **VO:** "Super admins oversee the entire platform — every plant, every account. That
  power is protected by two layers of security. Here's how to sign in safely."
- **Music:** Low, secure pulse.

## SCENE 2 — WHO / PREREQUISITE (0:25 – 0:55)
- **Screen capture:** `/login` → **Plant Staff** tab (super admins sign in here too).
- **On-screen text:** `For allow-listed authority accounts · password + email access`
- **VO:** "This is strictly for allow-listed authority accounts. You'll need your admin
  password and access to your email for the second factor. Both are required — by design."
- **Music:** Beat enters, restrained.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: ENTER YOUR ADMIN EMAIL (0:55 – 1:30)
- **Screen capture:** On the Plant Staff tab, type the authority email; the app detects
  a **password** method and reveals a password field.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Enter admin email → password field appears`
- **VO:** "Open the Plant Staff tab and enter your admin email. Because your account is an
  authority account, Concrete King recognizes it and asks for a password — unlike standard
  staff, who use codes only."
- **Cue:** Highlight the email field; password field slides in.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: ENTER YOUR PASSWORD (FACTOR 1) (1:30 – 2:10)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of a vault tumbler / padlock first stage clicking — layered security.
- **Screen capture:** Type the password; submit; server verifies the first factor.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Password = first factor`
- **VO:** "Enter your password. This is your first factor. If it checks out, Concrete King
  doesn't let you straight in — it moves you to the second layer."
- **Music:** Tension tick.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: ENTER THE 2FA CODE (FACTOR 2) (2:10 – 3:00)
- **AI B-roll:** Second padlock stage clicking open; two locks now aligned.
- **Screen capture:** A 2FA code is emailed; enter it on the verification step; the app
  calls the super-admin verify and issues the final session.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — Enter the emailed 2FA code = second factor`
- **VO:** "A one-time two-factor code is sent to your email. Enter it to confirm it's
  really you. Only when both factors match does Concrete King grant your admin session."
- **Music:** Resolve into a confident pulse.

## SCENE 6 — LAND IN THE CONTROL CENTER (3:00 – 3:30)
- **Screen capture:** Arrive at the **Super Admin / Platform Control Center** —
  plants, accounts, oversight.
- **On-screen text:** `You're in — Platform Control Center`
- **VO:** "You land in the Platform Control Center, with oversight across every plant and
  account on Concrete King."
- **Cue:** Sweeping reveal of the control center.

## SCENE 7 — SECURITY BEST PRACTICES (3:30 – 4:05)
- **On-screen text:** `Keep password private · protect your email · never share 2FA codes`
- **VO:** "With this level of access, security is on you too: keep your password private,
  protect the email that receives your codes, and never share a two-factor code with
  anyone — no one from the platform will ever ask for it."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 8 — RECAP & CLOSE (4:05 – 4:30)
- **AI B-roll:** The operations room lights settle; the map glows steadily.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Admin email` → `2 Password` → `3 2FA code` → `In`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "Email, password, then your two-factor code — three steps, fully protected.
  That's secure super-admin access on Concrete King."
- **Music:** Secure resolve, fade.
