# Tutorial 02 — Customer Email / Google Sign-In (Clerk SSO)

**Audience:** Customers / clients who prefer Google or email
**Runtime target:** 3:00–3:45
**Goal:** Teach a customer to sign in with a Google account or verified email.

> Spec inherited from `README.md`. Music: bright, modern, frictionless feel.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:20)
- **AI B-roll:** Clean cinematic shot of a phone unlocking with a face-ID glow; a
  laptop on a site office desk catches morning light. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `Prefer Google or email? Sign in in one tap`
- **VO:** "If you'd rather not deal with codes, you can sign in with the Google account
  or email you already use. It's fast, secure, and there's nothing new to remember."
- **Music:** Bright pad rises.

## SCENE 2 — WHO / WHAT YOU NEED (0:20 – 0:40)
- **Screen capture:** `/login` page, **Customer** tab, showing **Sign in with Google**
  and **Sign in with Email** buttons.
- **On-screen text:** `You'll need: your Google account or an email you can verify`
- **VO:** "This is for customers who want a one-tap sign-in. You'll just need your Google
  account, or an email address you can verify."
- **Music:** Light beat enters.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: CHOOSE GOOGLE OR EMAIL (0:40 – 1:15)
- **Screen capture:** Highlight the two buttons; tap **Sign in with Google**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Tap "Sign in with Google" (or Email)`
- **VO:** "On the Customer tab, choose how you'd like to continue — Sign in with Google,
  or Sign in with Email. We'll follow Google here; email works the same way."
- **Cue:** Tap-ripple on the Google button.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: VERIFY YOUR IDENTITY (1:15 – 2:00)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of a secure padlock forming from light particles — trust motif.
- **Screen capture:** The secure sign-in modal opens; pick a Google account / enter the
  email; complete the quick verification.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Pick your account & verify securely`
- **VO:** "A secure window opens. Choose your Google account, or enter and verify your
  email. Your sign-in is handled through a trusted, encrypted layer — your password
  never touches the concrete app itself."
- **Music:** Confident swell.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: LAND IN YOUR ACCOUNT (2:00 – 2:40)
- **Screen capture:** The modal closes, the app matches you to your customer account
  (or creates one), and you arrive at the **discovery / home dashboard**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — You're matched to your account → done`
- **VO:** "Once you're verified, Concrete King matches you to your customer account — or
  sets one up instantly if you're new — and drops you right into your dashboard."
- **Cue:** Smooth teal transition into the dashboard.

## SCENE 6 — TIPS / GOOD TO KNOW (2:40 – 3:10)
- **On-screen text:** `Tip: use the same Google/email each time · works alongside phone sign-in`
- **VO:** "A quick tip — always use the same Google account or email so you return to the
  same profile. And if you ever want to, you can still sign in with your phone number
  instead; it's the same account either way."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 7 — RECAP & CLOSE (3:10 – 3:45)
- **AI B-roll:** The customer closes the laptop, satisfied; a finished structure glows
  behind them.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Tap Google/Email` → `2 Verify` → `3 You're in`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "That's all there is to it — tap Google or email, verify once, and you're in.
  Welcome to Concrete King."
- **Music:** Warm resolve, fade.
