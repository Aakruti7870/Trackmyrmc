# Tutorial 05 — New Customer Registration

**Audience:** First-time customers creating an account
**Runtime target:** 3:30–4:00
**Goal:** Teach a new customer to create an account and sign in immediately.

> Spec inherited from `README.md`. Music: welcoming, optimistic, "first day" energy.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPEN / HOOK (0:00 – 0:25)
- **AI B-roll:** A new customer walks onto a fresh construction plot at sunrise, plans
  in hand, looking ahead with intent. Slow push-in. *(+ global style)*
- **On-screen text:** `New here? Create your account in under a minute`
- **VO:** "First project, first order? Getting started on Concrete King takes less than a
  minute. Let's create your account together."
- **Music:** Warm pad rises.

## SCENE 2 — WHO / WHAT YOU NEED (0:25 – 0:50)
- **Screen capture:** `/login` → tap **Create Account** (or open `/register`).
- **On-screen text:** `You'll need: name, company, email, phone (GST & city optional-ready)`
- **VO:** "This is for brand-new customers. Have your name, company, email, and mobile
  number ready — and your GST and city if you'd like them on file for billing."
- **Music:** Light beat enters.

## SCENE 3 — STEP 1: OPEN THE REGISTRATION FORM (0:50 – 1:20)
- **Screen capture:** From the login page, tap **Create Account**; the registration form
  opens.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 1 — Tap "Create Account"`
- **VO:** "From the sign-in screen, tap Create Account. You'll see a short form — nothing
  complicated, just the basics so the plant knows who they're serving."
- **Cue:** Tap-ripple on Create Account.

## SCENE 4 — STEP 2: FILL IN YOUR DETAILS (1:20 – 2:10)
- **Screen capture:** Fill name, company, email, phone, then a password; show optional
  GST and city fields.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 2 — Enter your details + a password`
- **VO:** "Enter your name and company, your email and mobile number, and choose a
  password. Add your GST number and city if you want them ready for invoices later. Take
  your time — accurate details mean smoother orders down the line."
- **Cue:** Highlight each field as it's mentioned.

## SCENE 5 — STEP 3: ACCEPT TERMS & SUBMIT (2:10 – 2:50)
- **AI B-roll:** Macro of a hand checking a clean toggle; a subtle teal confirm glow.
- **Screen capture:** Tick **Terms** and **Privacy Policy**, then tap **Create account**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 3 — Accept Terms & Privacy → Create account`
- **VO:** "Read and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy, then tap Create account. That's
  the last step — there's no waiting around for approval as a customer."
- **Music:** Confirm "ping".

## SCENE 6 — STEP 4: YOU'RE SIGNED IN (2:50 – 3:25)
- **Screen capture:** Account is created and you're logged in instantly, landing on the
  **discovery / home dashboard**.
- **On-screen text:** `Step 4 — Account created → you're in automatically`
- **VO:** "Your account is created and you're signed in right away — straight to your
  dashboard, ready to find a nearby plant and place your first order."
- **Cue:** Smooth teal transition into the dashboard.

## SCENE 7 — TIPS / GOOD TO KNOW (3:25 – 3:45)
- **On-screen text:** `Next time: sign in with phone OTP or Google/email — same account`
- **VO:** "One handy note for next time — you can come straight back in with a phone code
  or with Google and email. It all leads to this same account."
- **Music:** Pad softens.

## SCENE 8 — RECAP & CLOSE (3:45 – 4:00)
- **AI B-roll:** The customer smiles at the phone; a mixer rolls in behind.
- **On-screen text (build):** `1 Create Account` → `2 Your details` → `3 Accept & submit` → `4 You're in`
  then **CONCRETE KING — trackmyrmc.com**
- **VO:** "Create account, add your details, accept and submit — and you're in. Welcome to
  Concrete King."
- **Music:** Warm resolve, fade.
