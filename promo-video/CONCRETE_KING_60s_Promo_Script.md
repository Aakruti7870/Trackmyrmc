# CONCRETE KING — 60-Second Cinematic Launch Promo

**Product:** CONCRETE KING — Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) Marketplace & Plant Management
**Web:** trackmyrmc.com
**Runtime:** 60 seconds
**Aspect:** 16:9 master (with 9:16 + 1:1 crops noted for social)
**Tone:** Epic, modern, trustworthy — "nation-building meets precision engineering"

---

## CREATIVE CONCEPT — "From Plant to Pour"

Concrete is the foundation every city is built on — but the way it's ordered,
dispatched and tracked is still stuck in phone calls and paper challans.
CONCRETE KING turns that chaos into one command center: discover an approved
plant, order in taps, and watch every truck from batching to the final pour.

The film mirrors the journey of a single batch of concrete — **raw material →
batching → loaded mixer → live on the road → poured on site** — and overlays the
app as the invisible control layer guiding it all.

**Visual signature:** wet-concrete greys + steel, cut through with the brand
**teal-green** glow of the UI. The app screens "float" as clean glass panels over
real-site footage.

## DESIGN & AUDIO SPEC

- **Brand color:** Teal-green `#178a6e` (primary), deep teal `#0f2e29` (text/dark),
  emerald `#1f9e80` (accent glow). Neutrals: concrete grey `#9aa3a0`, off-white `#eaf6f1`.
- **Typography:** Display — a strong geometric/condensed sans (e.g. *Anton* or
  *Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold*) for impact; Body/UI — *Inter*.
- **Music:** Cinematic build — low pulsing drone → rhythmic percussion as features
  hit → triumphant resolve on the logo. ~120–128 BPM in the montage section.
- **VO voice:** Warm, confident, grounded male or female — measured, not hype-y.
  Indian-English friendly. Pace ~145 wpm so all lines breathe in 60s.
- **SFX:** mixer drum rumble, hydraulic hiss, UI ticks/whooshes on each screen,
  a single deep "lock-in" hit on the logo reveal.

## SHOT-BY-SHOT SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN / THE HOOK  (0:00 – 0:07)
- **Visual:** Slow-motion dawn over a massive construction site. A transit mixer's
  drum rotates, backlit by sunrise. Dust drifts. Camera pushes in low and heroic.
- **On-screen text:** *(none yet — let the image breathe)*
- **VO:** "Every great structure begins the same way…"
- **SFX/Music:** Low drone, distant site ambience, the deep rumble of the drum.

### SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM  (0:07 – 0:15)
- **Visual:** Quick, slightly desaturated cuts — a manager juggling phone calls,
  a stack of handwritten paper challans blowing in the wind, a truck idling, a
  clock ticking. Frames feel cluttered and tense.
- **On-screen text:** `Endless calls.` → `Lost paperwork.` → `Untracked trucks.`
  (each phrase snaps in on a hard cut)
- **VO:** "But ordering it, dispatching it, tracking it — that's where the day gets lost."
- **SFX/Music:** Tension rises; phone rings, paper rustle, a stressed percussive tick.

### SCENE 3 — BRAND REVEAL  (0:15 – 0:22)
- **Visual:** Everything snaps to clean. The clutter dissolves into a teal-green
  grid of light. The CONCRETE KING logo forms from a single poured "stroke" of
  concrete that hardens into the wordmark.
- **On-screen text:** **CONCRETE KING** / sub: *RMC Marketplace & Plant Management*
- **VO:** "Now there's one platform that runs it all. Concrete King."
- **SFX/Music:** A clean "whoosh + lock-in" hit. Music opens up, hopeful.

### SCENE 4 — DISCOVER & ORDER (MARKETPLACE)  (0:22 – 0:31)
- **Visual:** A phone floats forward. A map blooms with pins of nearby **approved**
  RMC plants inside a search radius. A customer taps a plant, selects grade **M25**,
  quantity, and pour time — order placed in seconds. Login shown as a quick
  **WhatsApp OTP** tap.
- **On-screen text:** `Find approved plants near you` → `Order in taps, not calls`
- **VO:** "Customers find a trusted plant nearby — and order the exact grade they
  need, in seconds."
- **SFX/Music:** UI ticks, satisfying confirm chime. Beat kicks in.

### SCENE 5 — LIVE TRACKING & DISPATCH  (0:31 – 0:41)
- **Visual:** Split-depth shot — a real mixer pulls out of the plant gate while,
  layered over it, the **live control-room map** shows that same truck moving in
  real time. A digital **challan** generates automatically. ETA counts down.
- **On-screen text:** `Live truck tracking` → `Auto-generated digital challans`
- **VO:** "From the moment it leaves the plant, every truck is live on the map —
  with digital challans and real-time ETAs."
- **SFX/Music:** Engine pull-away, GPS "ping" as the dot moves, momentum builds.

### SCENE 6 — QUALITY: MIX DESIGN & BATCH RECORDS  (0:41 – 0:48)
- **Visual:** Close-up of aggregate, cement and water — each measured precisely.
  Over it, the **Mix Design** screen shows clean proportion bars and the W/C ratio;
  the **Batch Report** logs the pour. A subtle nod to IS-code compliance.
- **On-screen text:** `Precise mix design` → `Every batch, on record`
- **VO:** "Every mix is designed to spec, and every batch is recorded — quality you
  can prove."
- **SFX/Music:** Crisp, precise ticks; clean and confident.

### SCENE 7 — CONTROL & INTELLIGENCE (DASHBOARD + AI)  (0:48 – 0:55)
- **Visual:** A sweeping pull-back reveals the **Dashboard** — KPIs, dispatch
  feed, analytics charts — as glass panels orbiting a 3D plant. The **AI assistant
  mascot** pops in and answers a question ("Cement for M25 per m³?") instantly.
- **On-screen text:** `Dashboards. Reports. AI assistance.`
- **VO:** "Plant managers see everything in one place — orders, fleet, reports —
  with an AI assistant that knows concrete inside out."
- **SFX/Music:** Music swells toward the peak; a friendly AI "chime".

### SCENE 8 — PAYOFF / LOGO LOCKUP  (0:55 – 1:00)
- **Visual:** Cut to the concrete being **poured on site** — smooth, perfect,
  finished. Pull up to a finished structure at golden hour. The logo settles
  center with the teal glow.
- **On-screen text:**
  **CONCRETE KING**
  *From plant to pour — perfectly tracked.*
  `trackmyrmc.com`
- **VO:** "Concrete King. From plant to pour — perfectly tracked."
- **SFX/Music:** Final triumphant hit, then resolve and fade.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Footage sources:** Real site/mixer B-roll (shoot or licensed stock) layered with
  clean screen-recordings of the live app for authenticity. Mixing real footage with
  the UI is what sells the "cinematic + real" feel you asked for.
- **Screen recordings to capture:** marketplace map + place-order flow, live tracking
  map, challan generation, mix design screen, dashboard, AI assistant reply.
- **Pacing:** Scenes 1–3 are slower and atmospheric; 4–7 cut faster on the beat;
  Scene 8 slows back down to land the brand.
- **Social crops:** For 9:16 (Reels/Shorts) keep text centered and trucks/phone
  vertical; for 1:1 tighten to the device + one headline per beat.
- **Length variants:** A 30s cut = Scenes 1, 3, 4, 5, 8. A 15s teaser = Scenes 3, 5, 8.
